Product Approach

How I take an AI product from an ambiguous problem to a measurable decision.

AI product management is not just feature delivery. The model behavior, quality threshold, human fallback, risk, and business case are part of the product.

Product layer

  • Define user problem, workflow, constraints, and success metric
  • Translate discovery into PRD, roadmap, backlog, and release criteria
  • Make tradeoffs explicit across impact, effort, risk, and adoption
  • Instrument KPIs and rollout evidence

AI quality layer

  • Design evals before declaring the model “good”
  • Inspect failure modes by category, not just overall accuracy
  • Set confidence thresholds and human-review routes
  • Build auditability, guardrails, and escalation into the workflow
