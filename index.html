Wells Fargo · U.S. Consumer Operations

GenAI complaint triage: under 2 seconds per complaint, 95% test accuracy

May 2025 to Feb 2026Senior Assistant Vice President, GenAI and AutomationPOC through production-readiness review

Problem

Manual triage took 3 to 5 minutes per complaint, and fraud categories carried the highest cost of error.

What I owned

Discovery, requirements and PRD, POC, evaluation, and the production-readiness review with risk, compliance and technology.

Result

95% test accuracy, under 2 seconds per complaint, and a USD $3M projected annual savings case.

Specified for the production-readiness review. The project was a POC; it was not deployed.

+Decisions and trade-offs
  • Tested precision and recall per category instead of relying on one accuracy number. That exposed weak results in low-volume, high-risk fraud categories.
  • Revised prompts and escalation thresholds for those categories before release sign-off.
  • Specified confidence-based human review, so uncertain cases go to a person, and full audit trails for explainability and CFPB requirements.
  • Also delivered the business case for a GenAI PDF-extraction project, coordinated the Microsoft Fabric (Azure) migration setup, and standardized Pre-Legal and Post-Judgment workflows across regulated jurisdictions.

How I deliver

Five habits I bring to every AI project.

  1. Start with the workflow

    The user, the business problem and what it costs today.

  2. Agree the bar first

    Scope, owners and acceptance criteria before the build.

  3. Keep risk visible

    Risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies in the open.

  4. Test by failure category

    Not one accuracy number. Human review and audit trails in the plan.

  5. Measure after go-live

    Adoption, quality and benefits, checked by the people who own them.
